# HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data to understand the key factors associated with employee attrition. The analysis was performed using **Microsoft Excel** for data cleaning and preparation and **Microsoft Power BI** for interactive visualization and dashboard development.

The main objective is to identify patterns in employee attrition based on factors such as department, job role, age, salary, overtime, job satisfaction, and years at the company.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Calculate the overall employee attrition rate.
* Analyze attrition across different departments and job roles.
* Understand the relationship between age and employee attrition.
* Analyze attrition based on salary groups.
* Analyze attrition based on employee experience/tenure.
* Investigate the impact of overtime on employee attrition.
* Analyze the relationship between job satisfaction and attrition.
* Create an interactive HR dashboard in Power BI.
* Generate insights that can help improve employee retention.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains employee-related information such as:

* Age
* Gender
* Department
* Job Role
* Monthly Income
* Job Satisfaction
* OverTime
* Years at Company
* Job Level
* Distance From Home
* Attrition

The target variable is **Attrition**, which indicates whether an employee left the organization.

* `Yes` → Employee left
* `No` → Employee stayed

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The data was prepared using Microsoft Excel.

The following steps were performed:

1. Checked for missing values.
2. Checked for duplicate records.
3. Verified data types.
4. Checked categorical values for consistency.
5. Created an **Age Group** column.
6. Created a **Salary Group** column.
7. Created an **Experience/Tenure Group** column.
8. Prepared the cleaned dataset for Power BI.

### Age Groups

* 18–24
* 25–34
* 35–44
* 45–54
* 55+

### Salary Groups

* Low
* Medium
* High
* Very High

### Experience Groups

* 0–2 Years (New Employee)
* 3–5 Years (Early Career)
* 6–10 Years (Experienced)
* 10+ Years (Highly Experienced)

## 📈 Key KPIs

The Power BI dashboard includes the following KPIs:

* **Total Employees**
* **Attrition Count**
* **Attrition Rate**
* **Average Age**
* **Average Monthly Income**
* **Average Years at Company**

### Attrition Rate Formula

```text
Attrition Rate =
(Employees Who Left / Total Employees) × 100
```
